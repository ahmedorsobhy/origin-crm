Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how OR for Real Estate Marketing & Brokerage (Ahmed Sobhy Sadek & Partner), trading as Origin Real Estate, Commercial Register 48840, Egypt ("Origin", "we") handles personal data collected through our Facebook and Instagram lead forms, our website and our internal CRM.

What we collect

The details you choose to send us in a lead form or message: your name, phone number, email if given, and your answers about the property you want to buy, sell or rent. We also keep a record of our calls and messages with you.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about the property request you made, to send you suitable units or buyers, and to follow up on that request. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Who can see it

Only Origin's own sales team and managers, inside our private CRM. Data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) under access controls. We share your details with another party (for example the other side of a deal or a developer) only when you ask us to, or when it is needed to complete your transaction.

How long we keep it

As long as your request is active and for up to 3 years afterwards for our records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time — see Data deletion.

Contact

Origin Real Estate — Sheikh Zayed, Giza, Egypt. Facebook page: Origin For Real Estate - OR.
